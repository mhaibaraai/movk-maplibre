## Introduction

`MaplibreMaskLayer` builds an inverted mask with the whole world as the outer ring and the outer rings of the target area as holes. `data` accepts Polygon, MultiPolygon, Feature, and FeatureCollection. `outline` also draws the area boundary, and data changes are applied incrementally through `setData`.

::note
Holes inside the target area (such as enclaves) are not masked and stay highlighted with the area.
::
